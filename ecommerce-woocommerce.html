Common WooCommerce Challenges

Where a plugin-heavy WordPress store starts to strain

🧩 Plugin Conflicts

Dozens of plugins stacked over time, breaking each other with every update.

🐢 Slow Under Load

Site speed and database performance degrading as catalog and traffic grow.

🎨 Theme Constraints

Page builders and off-the-shelf themes fighting custom design requirements.

🔌 Missing Integrations

No plugin covers your specific ERP, shipping, or pricing logic out of the box.

🔒 Security Debt

Outdated plugins and unpatched WordPress core creating real vulnerability risk.

📦 Migration Anxiety

Years of orders, customers, and SEO equity at risk during any platform change.
