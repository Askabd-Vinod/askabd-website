When Off-The-Shelf Platforms Stop Working

Signs your business model has outgrown Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento

🧱 Unique Business Model

Marketplaces, subscriptions, or B2B2C models that don't map to standard platform assumptions.

🔓 Vendor Lock-In

Growing dependency on a platform's roadmap, pricing, and limitations instead of your own.

🧩 Endless Workarounds

Stacking apps and custom code just to approximate features the platform was never built for.

📊 Complex Pricing Logic

Dynamic, contract-based, or negotiated pricing that generic rule engines can't express.

🔌 Deep System Integration

Core business systems that need to be first-class citizens of the storefront, not bolted on.

📈 Scale Beyond Platform Limits

Transaction volume or catalog complexity approaching what the platform was designed to handle.
