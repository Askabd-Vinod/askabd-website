Common Checkout Challenges

Why customers abandon their carts before completing a purchase

🛒 Complex Forms

Too many fields and steps driving customers away before purchase completion.

💳 Limited Payment Options

Customers leave when their preferred payment method is unavailable.

🔐 Security Concerns

Customers hesitate to enter card details on unfamiliar checkout pages.

📱 Mobile Friction

Checkout forms not optimized for small screens and touch input.

💰 Hidden Costs

Unexpected shipping or tax charges revealed only at the final step.

⏱️ Slow Processing

Long loading times between checkout steps causing drop-offs.
