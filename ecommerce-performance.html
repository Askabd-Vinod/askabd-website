Common Performance Challenges

Why slow stores lose customers and revenue

🌍 Slow Pages

Every 1-second delay costs 7% in conversions. Most stores load in 4-6 seconds.

🔐 Heavy Images

Unoptimized product images consuming excessive bandwidth.

❌ Bloated Code
